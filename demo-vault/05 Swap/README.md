# Swap

Swapping exchanges two things in one reversible operation, rather than making you move one out of the
way first. It works at three scales, and in every case both sides are locked while it runs so nothing
can drift half-way through.

| Note | What it covers |
| --- | --- |
| [17 Swap file](<./17 Swap file.md>) | Two notes trading places, links updated on both sides |
| [18 Swap folder](<./18 Swap folder.md>) | Two folders, optionally with their whole structure |
| [19 Swap selections](<./19 Swap selections.md>) | Two pieces of text, in one note or across two |
